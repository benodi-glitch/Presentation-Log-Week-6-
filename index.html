Group Presentation Listening Log

Audience Task — Business Life Group Presentations

How to use this log

Your group is the audience. Listen actively and record keywords or short phrases. Do not write a full summary.

1. Case: What real-life business event or incident did they present?
2. Cause → Effect: What important relationship did they explain?
3. Connection / Contrast: How did they connect the case to a homework example, or show a meaningful difference?
4. Takeaway: What useful insight can your group learn from the presentation?
